# Prompt templates for this visual language

These templates condense the Fablemind process. They are reusable instructions, not claims that every word appeared in the original historical prompt. For the verbatim original, consult Fablemind's `docs/svg-artwork-workflow.md` when available.

## 1. Composition reference

> Create a cohesive illustration composition for [subject/use]. Use original dimensional botanical and architectural illustration: strong silhouettes, sculptural arches, layered floating landscapes, expressive organic forms and clear negative space. Connect [narrative stages] with one continuous visual device. Use [approved palette]; for the Fablemind direction, iris and deep violet, coral, butter yellow and lilac. Keep all scenes in one coherent visual language, with large readable forms and deliberate perspective. Follow [aspect ratio and placement requirements].

Fablemind's signature was a waveform becoming a vine across seed, roots and flowering plant. That narrative device belongs to that brief. For a new subject, choose a device that carries its actual meaning.

The historic composition produced richer painterly shading than the final direction. A separate flattening edit established the final surface. Asking for the final finish in the first generation may save a pass, but verify it rather than assuming equivalent results.

## 2. Extract a transparent plate

> Use the attached approved composition/crop as the edit target. Recreate only [region] as a production illustration plate. Preserve its silhouette, perspective, palette, scale, placement and margins. Remove UI text, buttons, interface borders and page ground. Keep only the illustration and its own intentional shadows. Make outside space and interior openings genuinely transparent; keep white foreground objects opaque. Preserve the framing rather than enlarging the subject to fill the canvas. Return a transparent PNG.

Use the source crop as a reference, not a text description alone. Attach/inspect it using the image tool's supported edit mechanism.

## 3. Simplify the approved artwork

> Redraw this same image with moderately simplified, precise vector-style geometry. Preserve composition, aspect ratio, subject scale, placement, silhouette, palette and every major architectural and botanical feature. Remove brush grain, paper texture, speckles, scratches, chipped paint, tiny rock cracks, hatching and painterly highlights. Use smooth Bézier-like contours, larger uninterrupted color shapes, and two or three clean cel-shaded planes per object to retain depth. Simplify leaf veins and roots while preserving their direction. Keep transparent alpha outside the artwork and through architectural openings. Add no objects or text.

## 4. Local correction

> Change only [identified part and defect]. Preserve [liked silhouette, color blocks, branching veins, placement, scale]. Keep [explicit exterior/interior distinction]. Leave the surrounding composition unchanged.

For Fablemind's leaf, the eventual constraint was **smooth exterior with branching veins and color planes inside**. Earlier edits removed too much detail or cut notches into the exterior because those two concepts were conflated. Preserve that distinction in the prompt and verify a close-up before replacing the asset.
